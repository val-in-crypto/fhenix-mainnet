FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is Fhenix, in one line?

A layer that gives any token on Ethereum or Arbitrum a confidential mode, so balances and transfers can be hidden onchain and shown again when you choose.

What does "two modes" mean?

Public mode works like any ERC-20: balances are visible onchain. Confidential mode encrypts the same token with fully homomorphic encryption. You switch between them in one click, with no wrapper, no bridge and no new liquidity.

Where is it live?

Mainnet on Ethereum and Arbitrum from Oct 21, 2026, with five partner dApps on day one. CoFHE, the engine underneath, has been running on Ethereum Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia and Base Sepolia testnets.

Why "survives quantum computers"?

The encryption is lattice-based, the family of maths NIST selected for the post-quantum era. Most of today's crypto signatures will need to migrate. This design does not.

How do quests and swag work?

Do something on mainnet, post it on X, earn credits, spend them in the Fhenix shop. Quests run through the Snag widget on this page. Everyone who completes one in October is on the November allowlist.

I'm a developer. Where do I start?

Confidential logic is one Solidity import away. Docs, SDK and demos live under the Developer menu in the header, and the research is at fhenix.io/research.
