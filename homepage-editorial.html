05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little
more clarity.

What does “two modes” mean?+

Public mode shows information onchain. Confidential mode uses encryption to protect supported information. The demo above illustrates the difference; it does not move or encrypt real tokens.

Can I use any token?+

Availability depends on the token and application integration. Check the supported assets and network in the app you choose before using it.

Do I need a wallet for this demo?+

No. You can switch the visual demo between modes without connecting a wallet, signing a transaction or using funds.

When does mainnet launch?+

The planned launch is October 21, 2026, on Ethereum and Arbitrum. Follow Fhenix’s official channels for confirmed availability and application links.
